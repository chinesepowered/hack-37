The AI floor-plan auditor for equipment lenders

The problem
Floor-plan lenders finance dealers' inventory.
Dealer sells a unit, keeps the money: sold out of trust.
The classic floor-plan loss.
Caught by periodic field audits.
An auditor drives lot to lot with a clipboard, weeks after the fact.
How it works

Every lot. Every serial. Every night.

01
Browses every lot
An always-on Agent37 agent opens every dealer's public inventory in its own browser, every night.
Agent37 browser
02
Saves what it read
Every listing it read goes to listings.json on its own computer.
Agent37 Files API
03
Matches every serial
Every financed serial number, matched deterministically:
Verified Sold out of trust Not found Sale pending
04
Builds the case
For every exception:
  • Evidence: listing as published, URL, timestamp
  • Recovery value from live market comps
  • Payoff demand and credit memo drafted
Monid live comps
